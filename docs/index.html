A FEW THINGS YOU MIGHT BE WONDERING

Good questions.
Straight answers.

Do I need to know exactly what I want?

No. Start with what is frustrating you or what you want to improve. We can help shape a practical first step, whether that is a review, a focused change or a new build.

Can you work with my existing website?

Yes. A rebuild is not always the right answer. We can review what you have, identify what is worth keeping, and scope the improvements around your goals and platform.

Does the website check measure my conversion rate?

No. It checks signals in your public homepage HTML. Actual conversion rates need visitor and outcome data from your analytics or business systems. A useful next step is defining which enquiries, bookings or purchases to track.

How much does a project cost?

Each project is scoped around its requirements, complexity and support needs. We agree the scope and investment before work starts. There are no fixed prices or guaranteed results hidden in this website check.

What happens after launch?

We agree a handover and support plan with you. This can include hosting, maintenance, updates or further improvements, with responsibilities and ongoing costs made clear in the project scope.

YOUR INFORMATION

A clear approach
to privacy.

Website checks

Your website address is sent to our Supabase scanner, which retrieves the public homepage. Reports and page content are processed in memory and are not saved to our database. Short-lived, pseudonymous rate-limit counters help prevent abuse. Hosting providers may retain operational request logs. The scan does not access private pages or customer accounts.

Project enquiries

The enquiry form prepares a draft in your browser. It does not send your details to ScopeX until you send the email through your email app. Your email provider then handles delivery. Do not include passwords or sensitive customer information.

Your choices

This site adds no advertising trackers or analytics scripts. Download a report if you want to keep it; refreshing clears the report and form state. Questions about information you have emailed to ScopeX can be sent to bdesjardins@scopex.co.
